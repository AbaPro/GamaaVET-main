# GamaaVET — Progress Summary
**14 June – 1 November 2026**

## Highlights

- **New finance system**: money transfers now need approval before they happen, every safe, bank and personal account has its own history, and a new finance dashboard shows revenue, costs and balances.
- **Better data privacy**: each brand, salesperson and user sees only the customers, products, stores and accounts they are responsible for.
- **Customer portal upgrades**: staff review portal orders before accepting them, and customers can add notes to their orders.
- **Smarter manufacturing**: reusable formula templates, a password lock on formulas, and a sourcing list that shows which materials need to be bought.
- **Safer stock transfers**: stock moves between stores only after the receiver confirms it arrived.

## What's new by area

**Finance**
- Transfers need approval, and can be rejected
- Personal accounts, plus a full history for every account
- Finance dashboard and revenue / cost reports for any date range
- Multiple currencies, with exchange rates managed in a new Settings page
- Receipts and photos can be attached to payments and expenses
- Deleting financial records now needs a manager's approval
- Order pricing protected by a password

**Sales & Customers**
- Portal orders are reviewed before they are accepted
- Customers can leave notes on their orders
- Customer wallet balances can be corrected, and deposits go into the right safe or bank
- Each user sees prices only if they are allowed to

**Purchases**
- Easier creation and editing of purchase orders
- Photos of goods receipts can be attached
- Each payment records which safe, bank or personal account it came from
- Purchase orders can be filtered by payment status

**Inventory**
- Transfers go through send → receive → verify
- Low-stock alerts clear themselves once stock is refilled

**Manufacturing**
- Formula templates, plus uploading many formulas at once from a spreadsheet
- Formulas locked by password
- Sourcing list and planning for the materials needed
- Packaging photos and quality-check dates on orders

**Products**
- Units of measure, including volume
- Products can be archived and restored
- Units can be updated for many products at once

**General**
- Files can be attached several at a time across the system
- Export selected rows from any table to Excel / PDF
- A clearer activity log that shows who did what
- Admins can log users out remotely
- Tickets can be deleted by users who have permission

## Key fixes

- Stock was taken out of a store as soon as a transfer was created; it now moves only when the transfer is received
- Customer wallet deposits were not being added to the safe or bank; past deposits were corrected
- Deleting a payment no longer erases its money record, so the history stays complete
- Old low-stock alerts no longer stay open
- The same record can no longer get more than one deletion request at a time
- Several smaller fixes to purchase orders, payment history and buttons

## Coming next (in progress)
- Currency exchange between safes and bank accounts
- A Treasury Movements report showing all money moving in and out
