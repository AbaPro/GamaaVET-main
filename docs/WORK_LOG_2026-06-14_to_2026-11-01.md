## 2. Changes by category

### 2.1 New features

**Finance**
- **Transfers that need approval**: a new transfer stays *pending* and moves no money until the assigned approver approves it. Approvers can also reject. Old transfers were kept as approved records. *(2 Sep)*
- **Personal accounts** as a full account type, next to safes and banks. *(2 Sep)*
- **Detail pages with a full ledger** for each safe, bank account and personal account. *(2 Sep)*
- **Finance dashboard**: cash position, revenue, costs and balances. Date filters (this month, last month, this year, custom range) and quick buttons for transfers and expenses. *(10 Oct)*
- **Report detail pages** for revenue, COGS and material costs, with a shared reporting-period filter. *(13 & 17 Oct)*
- **Account balance adjustments** and a **"Settle balances"** permission for customers, vendors and personal accounts. *(16 & 22 Sep)*
- **Account deletion**: only allowed when the account has no balance and no linked records. A separate **force delete** permission skips this check. *(5 & 28 Sep)*
- **Deletion requests and approval** for finance records and manufacturing orders. Duplicate pending requests are blocked. *(7, 13 & 24 Oct)*
- **Image attachments on expense payments** and on finance transfers. *(9 Jul, 30 Sep)*
- **Safes have a currency and a location**, and the safes overview can be filtered by location. *(16 Sep)*
- **Order Pricing** module behind a password that only admins can set or change. *(17 Oct)*

**Sales & Customer Portal**
- **Review stage for portal orders**: orders placed in the customer portal are reviewed before they are accepted. Approved requests can be reopened for pricing. *(7–8 Sep)*
- **Customer order notes**: customers add notes from the portal, and staff see them in order details. *(2 Sep)*
- **Customer wallet balance adjustments**, and wallet deposits that credit the chosen safe or bank. *(30 Aug, 22 Oct)*
- **Currency rates and conversion** in sales orders and the portal, with a new **Settings** page to manage them. *(27 Oct)*
- **Tracking of manufactured products** in sales order details. *(25 Jun)*
- Customer order view shows prices, discounts and shipping, based on permissions. *(5 Oct)*

**Purchases**
- **PO receipt images**, with the latest receipt marked by a badge. *(2 & 12 Aug)*
- **Payment sources for PO payments**: each payment is linked to a safe, bank account or personal account. *(5 Sep)*
- **Transaction date** field on PO payments, item receipts and vendor-wallet entries, separate from the date the record was created. *(16 Sep)*
- New **"View PO details"** permission. *(2 Sep)*

**Inventory**
- **Transfer workflow**: the sender picks a receiver. The receiver **receives** the transfer (stock moves at this point) or **rejects** it with a reason. A manager then **verifies** it or sends it back for correction, which reverses the stock. Pending transfers can be edited. *(6 Aug)*

**Manufacturing**
- **Formula templates**: create, edit and delete, and use a template when editing a formula. *(3 Aug)*
- **Formula password**: formulas are unlocked with a shared password, and admins can reset it. *(5 Oct)*
- **Sourcing list and sourcing planning**: one combined list of materials for orders waiting for sourcing, with filters for customer, location, material source and search. *(22 Oct)*
- **Bulk upload of formulas** from CSV or Excel. *(30 Oct)*
- **Packaging and labeling images** in the manufacturing steps, and **quality-validation dates** in the order report. *(30 Oct)*
- Custom material and multiple photos per item on the dispatch checklist. *(28 Aug)*
- Required component quantities rescale automatically when a manufacturing order is edited. *(10 Aug)*

**Products**
- **Units** are required for raw materials (upload, create, edit, export, inventory views), and formulas check and convert units. *(17 Sep)*
- **Volume units**. *(22 Sep)*
- **Bulk update of units** for raw materials. *(22 Sep)*
- **Archive and restore** products. Archived products cannot be picked in orders, quotations or the portal. *(22 Sep)*
- Delete a product image while editing. *(8 Jul)*

**Tickets**
- **Delete tickets**, controlled by a new permission. *(4 Sep)*

**Platform**
- **Multi-file attachments** for finance transfers, inventory transfers, PO payments and the vendor wallet. Thumbnails share one component, and existing single files were copied into the new structure. *(15 Jul)*
- **Select and export rows** on data tables across the app. *(16 Sep)*
- **Structured activity log** with action type, record type and record ID, filtering, and CSV export. *(23 Sep, 7 Oct)*
- **Report catalog** in the Analysis module. *(28 Aug)*
- **Logo per brand** in the header, invoices and quotations. *(25 Jul)*

### 2.2 Improvements

- **Price visibility controls** in sales, purchases, the dashboard and order/PO summaries. *(8 Jul)*
- **Purchase order create/edit** reworked, with vendor details loaded into the PO form. *(25 Jun, 14 Aug)*
- **PO list**: filter by payment status, clickable stats cards, notes included in the export. *(2 Sep, 7 Oct)*
- **Exports**: shared export helpers. Invoices and quotations use HTML tables for cleaner PDFs. Manufacturing Excel keeps its formatting. Product export accepts several customer filters. *(7 Oct)*
- **Currency checks**: transfers and payments are blocked when the order's currency does not match the safe or bank. *(16 Sep)*
- **Vendor payables** calculation simplified. *(17 Oct)*
- Expenses can only use **active accounts**. *(16 Sep)*
- Safes and banks are **allowed to go negative**. Personal accounts still need enough balance. *(22 Oct)*
- Product search placeholders now say "search by name or SKU". *(29 Jul)*
- Inventory pages: product type is handled, alerts are clearer, sorting added. *(27 Jul, 10 Aug)*
- Formula editing requires a unit for every component. *(28 Aug)*
- Status dropdown with validation on the manufacturing order details page. *(28 Aug)*
- Customer is optional for raw and primary products. *(24 Sep)*
- After saving a product, the user goes back to the page they came from. *(22 Sep)*
- The Settings link moved to the bottom of the sidebar. *(30 Oct)*
- Large cleanup of the customer portal and other modules. *(7 & 23 Sep)*

### 2.3 Security & access control

- **Salesperson scoping**: customers, products, orders and quotations are limited to the customers and factories assigned to each salesperson. *(14 Jul)*
- **Brand/channel scoping** for customers, inventories, safes, bank accounts, notifications and finance data, so one brand cannot see another brand's data. *(15 Jul → 17 Sep)*
- **Inventory access checks** on add, upload, view and transfer. *(20 Jul, 10 Aug)*
- **Customer access checks** in manufacturing formulas and packaging options, and final products must belong to the current channel. *(30 Aug)*
- **Per-user safe and bank access**, managed from the user create/edit forms. *(7 & 16 Oct)*
- **Finance access by region**. *(17 Sep)*
- **Session management**: sessions can be revoked, revoked accounts are logged out, admins can force a logout, logout is CSRF-protected, and there is a session-status check. *(7 & 30 Oct)*
- **Login permission for factory users**. *(7 Oct)*
- **Permission cleanup for tickets**: users who can only update status can still open the ticket list. Ticket view uses prepared statements. *(14 & 28 Aug)*
- CSRF protection on deleting portal orders, plus delete and force-delete permissions for portal orders. *(17 Sep)*

### 2.4 Bug fixes

- **Inventory transfers removed stock as soon as they were created**, while their status still said "Pending". Stock now moves only when the receiver receives the transfer. *(6 Aug)*
- **Wallet deposits never credited the safe or bank**: manual customer-wallet deposits recorded a destination account but did not add the money to it. A one-time migration posted the past deposits, and the wallet page now credits the account directly. *(22 Oct)*
- **Deleting a payment deleted its finance transfer**. The transfer is now kept and marked as reversed, so the audit trail stays complete. *(2 Sep)*
- **PO details failed when the vendor contact or user was missing**. The query now handles missing records. *(17 Oct)*
- **Payment history missing for older payments**: PO payment loading now handles legacy records and checks permissions. *(17 Oct)*
- **Old low-stock alerts stayed open** after stock was refilled. They now clear automatically. *(10 Aug)*
- **Duplicate pending deletion requests** could be created for the same record. A unique constraint now prevents this. *(24 Oct)*
- **Vendor edit button stopped working** after the table was paged, sorted or filtered. *(14 Jul)*
- **Quick-transfer links from the finance dashboard** did not pre-select the account. *(10 Oct)*
- **Product SKU generation** used the wrong date format. *(16 Oct)*
- **Undoing a PO receipt** and receiving items fixed. *(14 Aug)*
- **Payment history could show another brand's accounts**. It now respects account scope. *(17 Sep)*
